| 3                                 | Point the way                        | >   | Shen Jen Zu             | 3   | 仙人指路            | Form Movement: Step Up and Point Sword Finger  <br>Hand Transition: Push the sword forward and to the left with both hands, then separate them.  <br>  <br>Right Hand (Sword Finger): Form a sword finger with your right hand. Circle it backward and down toward your hip, then continue the circular motion up near your right ear. Point the sword finger outward and upward at a 45-degree angle, finishing at eye height.  <br>  <br>Left Hand (Holding Sword): While holding the sword, sweep your left hand in a forward circle (brushing motion), finishing with the sword held straight behind your arm/back.  <br>  <br>Footwork & Stance: Simultaneously, step your right foot forward to meet your left foot, settling into a crouching stance with both knees bent.                                                                                                                                   |
| 4                                 | Circle around the moon               | >   | Sun Won Hew Tao         | 4   | 三环套月            | Hand & Foot Coordination: Circle the left hand (holding the sword) from the back to a flat position in front, palm facing down. At the same time, circle the right sword-finger down to the hip, swing it backward and upward, and place it on top of the left forearm. Meanwhile, step the left foot up and bring the right foot next to it.  <br>  <br>Preparation: Bend both knees and separate the hands. Lift the left knee with the toes pointing down, both hands pointing forward.  <br>  <br>The Kick: Pull the toes up, withdraw the elbows, and kick out with the left foot while extending both arms outward.  <br>  <br>The Final Stance: Pull the left foot back, pressing it against the back of the right knee. Pivot your hips to the right, bring the left hand forward with the sword vertical (facing right), and raise the right arm into an "S" shape. Look directly at the tip of the sword. |
