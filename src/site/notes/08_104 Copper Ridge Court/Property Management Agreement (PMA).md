When negotiating a Property Management Agreement (PMA) as a property owner, your primary goal is to **protect your cash flow, restrict unapproved spending, and retain absolute control over your asset.** Management companies often use standardized, broker-friendly contracts designed to maximize their administrative fees while shifting operational risk onto you.
作为业主，在协商物业管理协议（PMA）时，您的首要目标应当是：**保障现金流、限制未经批准的支出，并确保对自身资产拥有绝对的掌控权。** 物业管理公司往往会采用标准化的、偏向代理方的合同文本；这类合同旨在最大化其管理费用，同时将运营风险转嫁给您。
  
Here is your masterclass executive guide to dominating PMA negotiations from the property owner’s side of the table.
这是一份旨在助您在物业管理协议（PMA）谈判中占据主导地位的专家级指南——专为业主方量身打造。
### 1. Shift the Core Economics in Your Favor
扭转核心经济形势，使其向你有利的方向发展
Brokers often bury secondary revenue streams in the fine print. To protect your net yield, enforce these core financial safeguards:
经纪商往往会将次要收入来源隐藏在细则条款中。为保障您的净收益，请落实以下核心财务防范措施：
  

- **Gross Collected Revenues vs. Scheduled Rent:** Never allow the management fee (e.g., 6%–10%) to be calculated on _scheduled_ or _projected_ rent. Explicitly write into the deal that management fees are calculated **strictly on Gross Collected Revenues** (actual cash collected from tenants). If a unit sits vacant or a tenant defaults, the manager should not get paid.
    - **实收总收入与应收租金：** 切勿允许管理费（例如 6%–10%）基于“应收租金”或“预计租金”进行计算。应在协议中明确规定，管理费必须**严格按照“实收总收入”**（即从租户处实际收到的现金）来计算。若房源空置或租户违约，则管理方不应获得管理费。
      
    
- **Ancillary Revenue Audit:** Clarify who keeps late fees, lease break fees, application processing fees, and pet rent. Standard manager agreements claim 100% of these. Negotiate a **50/50 split on late fees** or demand that all tenant-paid fees flow into the property account.
    - **辅助收入审计：** 明确滞纳金、提前退租违约金、申请处理费以及宠物租金的归属。标准的管理协议通常规定这些收入全部（100%）归管理方所有。建议就“滞纳金五五分成”进行谈判，或要求所有由租户支付的费用均应计入物业账户。
      
    
- **Leasing Fee & Renewal Fee Caps:** Instead of paying a full month’s rent for tenant placement every time, cap placement fees (e.g., 50% of 1 month's rent) and limit lease renewal fees to a flat rate (e.g., $200–$300) rather than a percentage.
    **租赁佣金与续约费上限：** 改变以往每次招租均需支付相当于一个月租金作为佣金的做法，转而设定招租佣金上限（例如：相当于一个月租金的 50%）；同时，将租约续约费设定为固定金额（例如：200 至 300 美元），而非按租金比例收取。
      
    

### 2. Lock Down Maintenance & Vendor Oversight
停机维护与供应商监管
Uncontrolled repairs will quickly erode your cap rate. You need strict operational guardrails:
若维修缺乏管控，将迅速侵蚀您的资本化率。您需要设立严格的运营保障机制：
  

- **The Maintenance Spending Cap:** Standard agreements give managers $500–$1,000 of authorization per incident without asking. **Lower the non-emergency repair threshold to $250 or $300.** Any non-emergency job exceeding this amount _must_ require your written approval (email/text).
    **维护支出限额：** 在标准协议下，管理人员在处理每起事件时，无需请示即可自行决定 500 至 1,000 美元以内的支出。**请将非紧急维修的授权限额下调至 250 或 300 美元。** 凡超过此金额的非紧急维修事项，*必须*事先获得您的书面批准（通过电子邮件或短信）。
      
- **Zero Vendor Markups:** Many management companies add a 10%–20% administrative surcharge on top of third-party contractor invoices (electricians, plumbers, roofers). Require **direct pass-through pricing** with itemized contractor receipts provided on your monthly statement.
    **零供应商加价：** 许多管理公司会在第三方承包商（如电工、水管工、屋顶维修工）的账单基础上，额外加收 10%–20% 的管理附加费。应要求实行“直接成本结算”（即不加价转嫁），并确保在您的月度对账单中附上承包商的明细收据。
      
- **Preferred Vendor Clause:** Insist that all contractors used on your property are licensed, bonded, and carry at least $1M in general liability insurance—or reserve the right to supply your own preferred vendors for major jobs.
    - **指定服务商条款：** 明确要求在您物业上开展工作的所有承包商均须具备相应资质、缴纳保证金，并持有保额至少为100万美元的一般责任保险；或者，针对重大工程项目，保留由您自行指定服务商的权利。

To command absolute authority in Property Management Agreement (PMA) deals, you need to understand where the hidden margins and real liabilities sit. A Property Management Agreement is a service level contract between an owner (client) and a management company (brokerage).

  

While novices only argue over the core monthly management fee, seasoned experts focus on fee structures, legal indemnification, signing authority, and termination mechanics.

  

Here is your masterclass on speaking and negotiating like a master of Property Management Agreements.

  

### 1. Command the Core Vocabulary

Using the right terms signals instantly that you know where money is made or lost behind the scenes:

  

- **Gross Collected Revenues vs. Scheduled Gross Income:** Always clarify what the management fee is calculated against. Experts demand that management fees are based only on _Gross Collected Revenues_ (money actually paid by tenants), never _Scheduled Gross Income_ (potential rent, including uncollected arrears or vacant units).
    
      
    
- **Leasing Fee / Tenant Placement Fee:** The flat or percentage fee charged to source, screen, and place a new tenant (typically 50% to 100% of one month's rent).
    
      
    
- **Lease Renewal Fee:** The fee charged when an existing tenant signs a contract extension (usually a flat $200–$500 or 25% of one month’s rent).
    
      
    
- **Reserve Fund / Working Capital Reserve:** An account held by the manager with the owner's funds (typically $500–$2,000 per single-family house) to cover immediate emergency maintenance without waiting for approval.
    
      
    
- **Maintenance Markup:** The percentage fee (often 10%–20%) a management company adds on top of third-party vendor invoices for organizing and oversight.
    
      
    
- **Indemnification & Hold Harmless Clause:** The section defining liability—determines when the property manager is protected from losses unless there is gross negligence or willful misconduct.
    
      
    

### 2. High-Yield Phrases to Project Expertise

Drop these phrases into negotiations with owners or management agencies to establish immediate leverage:

  

- _"Let’s review the **maintenance expenditure threshold**—I want to set a $300 cap on non-emergency repairs without explicit written owner authorization."_
    
      
    
- _"We need to verify if your fee calculation includes **ancillary revenue streams** like late fees, pet rent, and application processing fees, or if those are retained exclusively by the broker."_
    
      
    
- _"I want to negotiate a **30-day termination for convenience clause** without penalty, rather than waiting for annual contract expiration."_
    
      
    
- _"How are **vendor markups** handled? We prefer direct pass-through invoicing from licensed, bonded, and insured contractors with zero administrative surcharge."_
    
      
    
- _"Let's make sure the **indemnification scope** excludes manager gross negligence, statutory violations, or willful breach of fiduciary duty."_
