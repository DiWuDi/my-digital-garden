### Key Details to Evaluate a Property Management Company

To get a clearer picture of an unaccredited property management firm, look beyond the BBB and evaluate these key areas:

1. **Third-Party Review Aggregators:** Check platforms like Google Reviews, Birdeye, or Yelp. Look for trends in how long-term property owners and tenants describe their communication, responsiveness, and maintenance handling.
    
2. **State Licensing Boards:** Verify that the firm and its brokers hold active licenses through the state's Real Estate Commission. Check for any official disciplinary actions or public regulatory filings, which carry far more legal weight than a BBB profile.
    
3. **Complaint Handling:** Take note of how management responds to critical public reviews. A professional firm actively addresses concerns and offers constructive solutions.
