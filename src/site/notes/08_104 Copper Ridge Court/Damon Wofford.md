Comparing the two agreements, the **Damon Wofford Realty (DWR) Property Management Agreement is significantly better for the owner** than the Trihelm Properties agreement.

  

The DWR contract eliminates several of the most egregious, one-sided penalties found in the Trihelm agreement while offering better financial protections during vacancies and tenant defaults.

  

### Key Comparisons: Why DWR is Better for the Owner

|**Feature / Clause**|**Trihelm Properties Agreement**|**Damon Wofford Realty (DWR) Agreement**|**Winner**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Fees During Vacancy**|Charges a **$50 minimum monthly fee** per property even if the property is completely vacant and generating zero income.|Explicitly states: _"There is no monthly fee charged when the property is vacant or if rent is not collected."_|**DWR** (Saves you money when unrented)|
|**Maintenance Conflicts**|Explicitly routes maintenance through an affiliated company (_Cover Source Solutions_) in which Trihelm holds a financial interest.|No affiliated self-dealing clause; manager coordinates third-party vendors without built-in internal markups.|**DWR** (Transparent and avoids conflicts of interest)|
|**Insurance Claims**|Charges an exorbitant **20% fee** on the _total insurance claim amount_ if they help manage a claim.|No insurance claim percentage fee (only standard project oversight fees apply to major repairs).|**DWR** (Protects your insurance payout)|
|**Tenant Default / Guarantee**|Zero protection; owner absorbs all losses if a tenant breaks a lease early or defaults.|Includes a **Tenant Guarantee**: If a placed tenant defaults during the first 6 months, DWR will market and screen for a replacement tenant **with no lease-up fee charged**.|**DWR** (Provides a safety net)|
|**Evictions & Court Costs**|Charges an upfront eviction fee plus court costs, requiring you to handle separate legal tracking for judgments.|DWR will represent the owner in court to obtain a judgment and go through the entire process at **no extra cost** to the owner (excluding actual court fees).|**DWR** (Better legal support inclusion)|
|**Project Oversight Threshold**|Charges a 10% supervision fee on construction/turns exceeding **$5,000**.|Charges a 10% project oversight fee only on major repairs exceeding **$1,500**, _plus_ explicitly exempts Roofing and HVAC replacements from this fee.|**DWR** (Higher threshold for minor items, explicit exemptions for major systems)|

### The Only Trade-Offs to Keep in Mind

- **Leasing Fee Structure:** Trihelm charges 50–100% of one month's rent, whereas DWR charges a flat **$850** for marketing and securing a new tenant. Depending on your expected monthly rent (if rent is over $1,150–$1,700), DWR's flat fee may actually be cheaper, or comparable.
    
      
    
- **Cancellation Notice:** Trihelm requires **30 days** written notice to cancel, while DWR requires **60 days** written notice. The 30-day window in Trihelm's contract is slightly more flexible, but DWR's overall financial fairness heavily outweighs this difference.
    
      
    

### Conclusion

The **Damon Wofford agreement** is structured as a much more equitable partnership. It ensures the management company only gets paid their monthly management fee when _you_ are actually collecting rent, protects you from paying a 20% tax on insurance claims, avoids internal self-dealing on maintenance, and provides a 6-month tenant default guarantee.
