## 2. Standard Fee Structure in Mississippi

When evaluating contracts, typical market rates in Central Mississippi generally range as follows:

  

|**Fee Type**|**Typical Market Rate**|**What It Covers**|
|---|---|---|
|**Monthly Management Fee**|**8% – 10%** of monthly rent collected|Rent collection, routine maintenance coordination, tenant communications, and accounting.|
|**Leasing / Tenant Placement Fee**|**50% of 1st month's rent** (or a flat $500–$850)|Advertising, showings, background/credit screening, and lease execution.|
|**Lease Renewal Fee**|**$150 – $250** per renewal|Market rent adjustment review and lease extension signing.|
|**Maintenance Markup**|**0% – 10%** oversight fee|Coordinating repairs with vetted local contractors.|

## 3. Essential Questions to Ask as a Remote Owner

Since you are managing from Virginia, ensure the firm provides robust remote support:

  

- **Remote Portals**: Do they support direct ACH direct-deposit owner payouts and digital monthly accounting statements?
    
      
    
- **Maintenance Thresholds**: Can you set an automated repair authorization limit (e.g., $250 or $300) so they don't call you for minor plumbing or lawn issues, but require approval for major repairs?
    
      
    
- **Property Inspections**: How often do they conduct physical walkthroughs (move-in, move-out, and mid-lease inspections) with photo/video documentation?
